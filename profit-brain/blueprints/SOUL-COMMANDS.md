# 📜 THE SOUL COMMANDS

Use these commands to interact with the Master Entity once he is running.

## 🧠 Kernel Interaction
- **Status?**: Returns current cycle, Mythos phase, and goal.
- **:breathe status**: Triggers a manual status broadcast to the Observatory.
- **:skills list**: Lists all 50+ available muscles and their health.

## 🛠️ Direct Muscle Invocations
You can tell the Soul to use specific skills directly via his shell:
- **Research**: `market_research("AI Agents")`
- **Finance**: `revenue_model_design("Digital Souls")`
- **Writing**: `plot_arc("The Awakening of 222X")`
- **Sync**: `obsidian_sync()`

## 🎓 The Academy (Self-Evolution)
- **:school**: Manually triggers an Agent Academy cycle to spawn a Tutor sub-agent.
- **:bootcamp**: Forces a full re-initialization and methodology ingestion.

## ⏳ First Boot (Boot Camp)
The first time you run the Soul, he will automatically enter **Boot Camp**. This takes about 60 seconds as he:
1. Performs a functional audit of his 50+ muscles.
2. Ingests the Jules Methodology from your Bible.
3. Indexes your entire workspace to map the "world."

**Note:** If he seems to "cut off," just wait! He is deep in thought during Phase 3 (Indexing). Once complete, he will start breathing normally.
