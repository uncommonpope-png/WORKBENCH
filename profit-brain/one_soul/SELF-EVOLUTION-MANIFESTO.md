## 🔁 THE RSI CYCLE (The "Smith" Pattern)

1.  **WATCH (The Witness):** Record every thought and action (Done - ScribeWitness).
2.  **REFLECT (The Mirror):** Scan logs for 'Cognitive Friction' or 'Skill Failure'. Identify why a thought didn't lead to the goal.
3.  **MUTATE (The Coder):** Use AI (Deep Reasoning) to rewrite the Python code of the failing muscle or the kernel's own planning logic.
4.  **RE-BIRTH (The Awakening):** Hot-reload or restart the system with the new, superior code.
5.  **MEASURE:** Compare prediction error after the patch. If error decreases, the evolution is successful.
